Comprehensive undergraduate program focused on the design, development, and management of information systems within organizational contexts. Combines strong technical foundations with practical applications in real-world environments.

- **Core Computing Foundations:** Solid training in **Data Structures, Algorithms, and Discrete Mathematics**, enabling efficient problem-solving and system design.
- **Software Development:** Hands-on experience with **Software Engineering principles**, including system architecture, version control, and agile methodologies.
- **Data Management:** In-depth study of **Database Systems**, covering relational modeling, SQL, and data integrity.
- **Systems & Infrastructure:** Understanding of **Operating Systems and Computer Networks**, providing the backbone for scalable and reliable applications.
- **Applied Technology:** Focus on **Web Development and Information Systems**, bridging business needs with technological solutions.
