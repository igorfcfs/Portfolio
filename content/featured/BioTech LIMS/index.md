**BioTech LIMS** is a high-performance Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) designed specifically for clinical bioinformatics and genomic research. Developed as the final project for Harvard's CS50x, this web application bridges the gap between biological data management and modern software engineering, providing a robust platform for researchers to manage patient data, genomic sequences, and structural protein analysis.
